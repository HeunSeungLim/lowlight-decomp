\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Extreme-dark enhancement scores a short-exposure frame's output against a long-exposure
reference. Those scores are not comparable across papers: reference-mean rectification, the eight-bit rule, how PSNR is aggregated and the benchmark rendition each move a score, the largest by nearly ten decibels and by five among paired-supervised methods, so we rerun every baseline under one protocol and price each choice on the pairs it applies to. An improved
score still does not identify the corrected error component.

We couple global, per-channel and spatial least-squares oracles with radial-band
input--output correlation, applying the procedure to \nCMPnAll{} reproduced
method--benchmark pairs on Sony sRGB and LOL. Noise and residual-donor controls probe spatial headroom; repeated captures support noise adjustment and frame-averaging tests. These measurements diagnose specified correction families.

Related work studies the effect of enhancement on downstream
consumers~\cite{empirical2024,limeeval2024}, and introduces Retinex
decompositions~\cite{kind2019,uretinex2022}, zero-reference
curves~\cite{zerodcepp2022}, normalising flows~\cite{llflow2022}, colour and Fourier
representations, signal-to-noise gating and
transformers~\cite{cidnet,cidnetplus,fecnet2022,fourllie2023,snrnet2022,llformer2023},
and diffusion priors~\cite{gsad2023,lightendiff2024}.
Brightness mismatch motivates mean-matching losses~\cite{gtmean2025}; burst pipelines
merge by frequency-dependent noise variance~\cite{hdrplus2016} or learn from dark
bursts~\cite{karadeniz2021}.

Oracle analyses have a history in denoising~\cite{chatterjee2010,levin2011}, error decomposition in detection~\cite{hoiem2012} and ring correlation in image assessment~\cite{frc2022}. Closest to us is the least-squares photometric--structural split of~\cite{pal2026}, proved orthogonal there and made into a training loss by affine colour alignment, a photometric term containing our global and per-channel gains. We instead read that split off frozen checkpoints as a diagnostic, and the correction it prescribes needs no reference at inference. We connect these band diagnostics to component-specific oracles, structured controls and repeated-capture adjustment, linking a score gap to a correction family whose predictability can be tested and, for the global term, exploited: one reference-free division whose precondition is measurable before use, worth \nFRetgain{}\,dB where it holds and costing \nLOLRFloss{}\,dB where it fails, and we report both.
